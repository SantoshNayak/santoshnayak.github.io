# Surface brief: portfolio (whole site)

Mode: Experience (a portfolio: the work leads, the interface recedes).

## Audience, job, proof, constraints
Recruiters and hiring managers skim for who Santosh is, what was delivered, and how to get in touch. Proof on hand: five roles with specific outcomes, skills, nine projects, thirteen published apps, certifications, seminar talks with video, two degrees (Physics Honours then MCA). Fixed: hero copy and tagline, Buy Me a Coffee link, Tawk.to embed (currently commented out in index.html; leave as is). No invented testimonials or metrics. Neon green/cyan gradient identity is released and must go.

## Chosen world and memorable moment
A mid-century physics-education poster world: flat silkscreen inks, ripple-tank interference as the carrier. The memorable moment: two wave sources labelled Physics and Code interfere on the hero canvas and the Code source follows the pointer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One idea: physics and code are two wave sources, and what a recruiter hires is the interference between them. Refuses the category default of dark hero, typing line over photo, three equal cards, glow accents.

OWN-WORLD: Ultramarine ground (#1a2bc4) drenches hero, talks, and contact; vermilion (#ff4b1f) is the second silkscreen ink, used for fills and rings, never body text; cool blue-tinted paper (#eef0f7) carries reading sections; blue-black ink (#0c1033) sets text. Flat inks only, no gradients, no glow, overprint by multiply. Archivo throughout: wide heavy stretch for display, normal width for text (Atkinson Hyperlegible was tried and dropped for its slashed zeros in the metrics). Hairlines, ruled ledgers, one registered rail, caption bars in poster grammar. Recognizable with all content removed by concentric-ring moire on ultramarine.

STORY: The visitor understands in one screen that this is an engineer who came from physics, sees five roles of delivered outcomes, skills grouped by job, projects and apps as shipped evidence, and reaches out via a visible Hire Me / Email action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height ultramarine. Top ~60%: canvas of two ring sources (paper-white rings from Physics, vermilion rings from Code) with small source labels; fixed hairline nav above. Bottom: a poster caption bar in blue-black ink (an adaptation of the full-ultramarine plan, kept so the name reads at full contrast) holding SANTOSH NAYAK in wide heavy caps at left, the rotating role line beneath, the two taglines and the Hire Me and Buy Me Coffee buttons at right. Primary action sits in the caption bar, high contrast.
